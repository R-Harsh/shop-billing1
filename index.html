🏷️ Bill discounts (based on bill amount)

Set up to 3 discounts. When the bill amount (before discount) goes above the amount you set, that discount % is given. Example: Bill above 3000, Discount 10 means 10% off on bills over ₹3000. Set Discount to 0 to hide one. Only one discount can be used on a bill at a time.

Bill above ₹Discount %

The discount is applied automatically when the bill goes above the amount you set. If a bill qualifies for more than one, the bigger saving is used. On the Bill screen, staff can type a different discount or clear the Discount box to remove it for that bill.

🎁 Complimentary products (based on bill amount)

Set up to 3 free gifts. When the bill amount (before discount) goes above the amount you set, the free product you choose can be added to the bill. Example: Bill above 1000, Snake crackers, Qty 1. Type any product name (suggestions from your item list appear as you type). If the name matches an item in your list, its stock goes down; any other name is simply written on the bill as FREE. Leave the name empty to hide a row. If the bill reaches a bigger amount, only that bigger gift is offered. Free items are shown as FREE on the bill.

Bill above ₹Free productQty

Free gifts are never added automatically. Staff taps “Give free” on the Bill screen.
